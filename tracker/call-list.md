threshold and still at `New`. None of them can be chased by email:

- **Server block** means their mail server refuses outside senders outright.
  Re-sourcing the address changes nothing — Anderson Automotive bounced 9 of 9

Every further email to these addresses adds to a hard-bounce rate already at
~14%, which is what gets a sending domain filtered. So these are phone calls or
they are nothing.

## What to actually say

These people were emailed once and never saw it — the mail never landed. So this
is a first contact, not a chase. Do not open with "I followed up a couple of
times"; they have no idea who you are.

The dealer groups are the bulk of this list, and they share a shape: multiple
sites, general sales managers with their own budgets, and a customer-event habit
already in place. The ask that fits is a single outing, not a partnership tier.

## Worth fixing at source

Eight companies is a small number to be permanently unreachable. Two of them —
Leith Cars and Sport Durst — are marked *stale* rather than *blocked*, meaning
the addresses are simply out of date. A fresh ZoomInfo pull on those two would
likely restore email as a channel; the other six will not improve.
