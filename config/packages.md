prospect. Prices are placeholders Tyler can set — tell me the real numbers and

| Package | Price (placeholder) | Who it fits | Core inclusions |
|---------|--------------------:|-------------|-----------------|

| **Stay & Play** | add-on / variable | Out-of-town HQ or travel-driven prospect | Golf + lodging package; layer onto any tier |

## How the workflow picks one (the Golf Fit logic)

Recommend the **highest tier the prospect realistically supports**, judged on
marketing budget first, then revenue, then fit:

- **Official Partner** — marketing budget ≥ $15M **or** revenue ≥ $150M, and
  Partnership Score ≥ 80.
- **Premier Partnership** — budget ≥ $5M **or** revenue ≥ $50M, score ≥ 68.
- **Marketing Partner** — budget ≥ $2M **or** revenue ≥ $20M.
- **Boutique Outing** — below that **but** in a relationship/hospitality
  industry (legal, financial, insurance, auto, commercial real estate) — lead
  with client entertainment, not signage.
- **Single Sponsored Event** — everything else (small local, awareness-only).
- **Stay & Play** — flag as an add-on whenever HQ is >60 min from the club or
  the prospect is travel/tourism-driven.

Always pair the recommendation with a one-line **why** (the factors that drove
it) and a **Golf Fit** band from the Partnership Score (see `scoring.md`):
Elite 85–100 · Strong 70–84 · Good 55–69 · Developing < 55.
