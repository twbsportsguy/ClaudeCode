# Finley partnership packages — the offer ladder

Source of truth for the **Recommended Package** the workflow attaches to every
prospect. **Prices and inclusions are mirrored from the Finley Golf Club
Package Builder** (`package-builder.html`, on the `finley-golf-package-builder`
branch). The Package Builder stays the separate, canonical quoting tool — this
file just reflects its corporate tiers so the tracker and dashboard can
recommend one. If the builder's prices change, update them here too.

## Corporate partner tiers (the three fixed packages)

| Package | Price | Who it fits | Core inclusions (from the builder) |
|---------|------:|-------------|------------------------------------|
| **Official Partner** | $40,000 | Full brand ownership + premium events; large regional/national presence, big marketing budget | 80-player outing · Night on the Range · 36-player boutique outing · 80 hosted rounds · $2,500 F&B credit · $1,000 merch credit · Premium marketing exposure · Football, basketball & 80 Olympic-sport tickets |
| **Premier Partner** | $27,500 | Balanced exposure + entertainment; strong mid-market company that entertains clients *(most popular)* | 80-player outing · Night on the Range · 16-player boutique outing · 60 hosted rounds · $1,000 F&B credit · Standard marketing exposure · Basketball + 60 Olympic-sport tickets |
| **Gold Partner** | $17,500 | Entry-level partnership access; solid local/regional brand | 80-player outing · 24 hosted rounds · Basic marketing exposure · 40 Olympic-sport tickets · Watch party |

## À-la-carte starters (for prospects below Gold)

When a prospect can't yet support a fixed tier, lead with a single builder item
sized to their goal — it's the low-friction way in:

- **Boutique outing (16 players)** — $3,500 · relationship/hospitality firms who
  mainly want client entertainment.
- **Boutique outing (24 players)** — $5,500 · team-building / prospect events.
- **Standard marketing** — $4,500 · awareness-first local brand (cart
  advertising ≈ 100k impressions/yr, email, clubhouse signage).
- **Basic marketing** — $1,500 · smallest awareness play (clubhouse + web logo).
- **Summer Series sponsorship** — $5,000 · own a recurring social event; great
  for reaching UNC community + Triangle young professionals.

Full à-la-carte menu (rounds, events, perks, marketing, Carolina athletics
tickets) lives in the Package Builder — point Tyler there to customize a quote.

## How the workflow picks one (Golf Fit logic)

Recommend the **highest tier the prospect realistically supports**, judged on
marketing budget first, then revenue, then fit:

- **Official Partner ($40,000)** — marketing budget ≥ $15M **or** revenue ≥
  $150M, and Partnership Score ≥ 80.
- **Premier Partner ($27,500)** — budget ≥ $5M **or** revenue ≥ $50M, score ≥ 68.
- **Gold Partner ($17,500)** — budget ≥ $2M **or** revenue ≥ $20M.
- **Boutique Outing ($3,500)** — below that **but** in a relationship/hospitality
  industry (legal, financial, insurance, auto, commercial real estate) — lead
  with client entertainment, not signage.
- **Standard Marketing ($4,500)** — everything else (small local,
  awareness-only) — the awareness-first entry point.

Always pair the recommendation with a one-line **why** and a **Golf Fit** band
from the Partnership Score (`scoring.md`): Elite 85–100 · Strong 70–84 ·
Good 55–69 · Developing < 55.
