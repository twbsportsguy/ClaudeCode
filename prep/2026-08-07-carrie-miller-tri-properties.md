## The pricing tension — the most important thing here

She asked about **Basic marketing, $1,500** (clubhouse + web logo). That is the
smallest thing on your menu.

By your own Golf Fit logic in `config/packages.md`, Tri Properties clears
**Gold Partner, $17,500** — the rule is budget ≥ $2M *or* revenue ≥ $20M, and
they are at $80.7M.

Both naive moves are wrong:

- Quote $17,500 to someone who asked about $1,500 and you lose the call.
- Sell $1,500 to an $81M firm and you have left the real opportunity untouched.

**The bridge is that she asked for the wrong product.** Their business is
brokers and tenant renewals — that is relationships, not awareness. A logo in
the clubhouse does very little for a commercial real estate firm. A boutique
outing does a lot, because their deals get decided socially long before the
paperwork.

So: answer her $1,500 question honestly, then tell her why you would point her
somewhere else. That is a more credible call than upselling, and it happens to
be true.

| Option | Price | The line to use |
|---|---:|---|
| Basic marketing | $1,500 | What she asked about. Clubhouse + web logo. Say plainly it is the smallest awareness play and probably not what moves their business. |
| **Boutique outing, 16 players** | **$3,500** | **Lead here.** Sixteen brokers or tenants on the course. Only $2,000 above what she asked about. |
| Boutique outing, 24 players | $5,500 | If they want clients *and* team in one day. |
| Gold Partner | $17,500 | 80-player outing · 24 hosted rounds · basic marketing exposure · 40 Olympic-sport tickets · watch party. The stretch — only if the CEO's interest turns out to be real. |

**Open decision that is yours, not mine:** whether basic marketing stays at the
listed $1,500 for an inbound $81M prospect, or whether you bundle it into the
$3,500 outing as the reason to go there instead. `Potential Revenue` in the
tracker is deliberately blank — fill it once she gives you a number.
